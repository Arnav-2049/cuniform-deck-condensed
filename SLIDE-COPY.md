> **⚠️ DECISION NEEDED — the headline.** Draft 1 read *"Code checking should happen while you design. Not after."* Fable's critique: "should happen" is a limp verb pair, and *"you design"* addresses an architect — J.R. has never designed anything. Two options above the line you locked:
> - **Used above:** *"Check the code while you draw. Not after you submit."* — active verbs, and "submit" is the moment J.R. actually prices.
> - **Bigger swing:** *"The city should never be the first one to check the code."* — names the villain, speaks pure permit language, lands directly on 30 years of his scar tissue. Stronger for him; less explicit about what the product does.
