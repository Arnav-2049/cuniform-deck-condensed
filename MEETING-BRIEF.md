## A. The hard ones

**"What's your accuracy rate?"**

What I can tell you today is how correctness is enforced — every flag is clause-linked, and we tune for zero false negatives on life-safety and accept false positives. **Do not invent a percentage.** Commit to a date for publishing the first benchmark.

**"Eighteen months and no revenue?"**
> "Eighteen months, zero outside capital, and a CTO doing it nights and weekends. In that time we shipped a working engine inside Revit and got two ENR-ranked firms to schedule testing. We deliberately didn't sell — selling a half-built compliance tool to a firm that stakes its license on accuracy is how you get one customer and no second one. This raise is the first time all of us are full-time."

**"What if you're wrong and a building fails?"**
> "Two parts. Legally, liability sits with the architect of record — licensing law creates a non-delegable duty, so responsibility for anything submitted under a professional seal cannot transfer to software. That's how the profession is structured, not our terms of service.
> But the real answer is that we're a second reader, not a replacement. Compare us to the status quo, not to perfection. Today an architect checks code against memory while drawing and gets a consultant's markup weeks later, if the budget covered a consultant at all. We add a check that didn't exist, at the moment it's cheapest to act on, with a citation trail. The failure mode we're replacing isn't 'perfect human.' It's 'no check until it's too late.'"

**"Why won't Autodesk just build this?"**
> "They monetize the platform and acquire point solutions. Being the best plug-in is both the strategy and the exit. And the hard part isn't the AI — it's jurisdictional depth across thousands of local amendments, which compounds slowly and unglamorously. That's a product company's job, not a platform's."

**"Only Revit?"**
> "Revit is where the work happens for the firms we're targeting. One integration done deeply beats three done shallowly, especially when accuracy is the product."

**"Does it do New York?"**

# PART 4 · CONCEPTS HE MAY REFERENCE

## Financing
| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **SAFE** | Simple Agreement for Future Equity. Not debt, no interest, no maturity. Converts to shares at the next priced round. 90% of pre-seed deals use them. |
| **Post-money cap** | The valuation ceiling at which your SAFE converts. $1.5M at $12M post = **12.5%** ($1.5M ÷ $12M). Post-money SAFEs make dilution certain and knowable — that is why 87% of them are post-money now. |
| **Pre-money vs post-money** | Pre-money = value before the investment. Post-money = pre-money + the money in. |
| **Dilution** | The share of the company you give up. Yours: 12.5% to this round, plus an option pool. |
| **Option pool** | Shares reserved for future employees, typically 10–15% at seed. **Watch this:** if it is carved out *pre-money*, the dilution hits founders, not the incoming investor. |
| **Vesting / cliff** | Standard is 4 years with a 1-year cliff. **Ask for credit for the 18 months already served.** |
| **Pro rata** | An investor's right to maintain their percentage in later rounds. |
| **Liquidation preference** | Who gets paid first in a sale. Not usually in a SAFE, but it appears at the priced round. |

## Metrics
| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **ARR / MRR** | Annual / monthly recurring revenue. Your M18 target: $1M ARR. |
| **TAM / SAM / SOM** | Total / serviceable / obtainable market. Yours: $8.16B market, ~$700M replaceable line item, 1,235-firm beachhead. |
| **Burn / runway** | Monthly spend / months until you are out. Yours: ~$80K/mo, 18 months. |
| **CAC / LTV** | Cost to acquire a customer / lifetime value. You are founder-led, so CAC is time not cash. |
| **NRR** | Net revenue retention. Expansion within existing customers. **Your $1M plan depends on this** — firm-wide expansion at 2–3 logos. |
| **Logo** | One customer company. |
| **Design partner** | An early customer who shapes the product, usually unpaid. Exactly what RAMSA and Hart Howerton are. |
| **LOI / MOU** | Letter of intent / memorandum of understanding. Non-binding. **Yours are verbal, not even written — do not upgrade the language.** |
