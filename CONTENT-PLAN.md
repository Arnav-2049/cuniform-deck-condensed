## SLIDE 2 · PROBLEM — *20 seconds*

**Job:** Make the problem undeniable using a number he cannot argue with, in his own economic language.

- **Headline number:** **40%** — *"Two in five permit submissions fail on the first review."*
  (~1.4M US permits/yr)
- **One line of consequence, in project-capital terms:**
  *"Each rejection adds 4–8 weeks. On a project of meaningful scale, that's **$50,000–$200,000 a
  month** in carrying costs — financing, taxes, insurance, deferred revenue."*
- **Optional third beat:** pre-construction runs ~12 months.

**Strip the old four-figure grid.** One number, one consequence.

> *Why:* The old slide led with a $2,500–15,000 consultant fee — an architecture firm's P&L line.
> He doesn't think in firm P&L; he thinks in carrying cost. He has paid that bill for thirty years.

**Sources:** ~40% first-submission rejection (industry analysis, ~1.4M permits/yr); carrying costs
1–3% of project cost/month, $50–200K/mo at meaningful scale; 12-month pre-construction (internal
research doc, corroborated).
